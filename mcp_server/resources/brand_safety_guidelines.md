# Pulseworks Marketing - Brand Safety Guidelines

1. **Ad Copy Limits**: Headlines must never exceed 50 characters in total length.
2. **Prohibited Terms**: Do not use absolute financial guarantees (e.g., "100% Guaranteed ROI").
3. **Tone**: Maintain a professional, actionable, and customer-focused voice across all ad platforms.
4. **Budget Modifications**: All daily limit modifications over $500 require human-in-the-loop approval.
